Yolitzy, better known as Yoli, is a mixed-media artist working across fiber, charcoal, photography, digital media, and web design. Born and raised in Lexington, KY, she is currently completing her B.A. in Art Studio at the University of Kentucky.

She began her practice with graphite and watercolor photorealistic work, discovered the Adobe Suite in high school, and has since evolved into making abstract, experimental multimedia art. Her work is rooted in personal experiences with mental health — bold, process-driven, and layered with traditional techniques, photography, and photo manipulation.

Yoli draws inspiration from childhood nostalgia and the DIY spirit found in independent fashion and music culture. Her goal is to build a community of like-minded creatives and to inspire young people through the power of self-creation and sustainability.

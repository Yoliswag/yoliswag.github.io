Hey, I’m Yolitzy — or Yoli.

I make things with textiles, take photos, design stuff, and often dig in my butt.
I’m scared of people and obsessed with music. I like making everything myself, usually in isolation, but I’m always looking for collaborators (or just someone to listen to me babber about).

I work as an artist assistant/photographer for a local artist and my other gig is designing, coding and studying AI generation for my professor. If you ever need custom fine jewelry, or help setting up a small website, online shop, or portfolio, I’d be happy to share what I know.
